# Portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: single-page portfolio. Mode: Experience (portfolio), recruiter-first.
Audience: recruiters for student SWE / AI roles. Action: open a project, read grades, email or LinkedIn.
Proof: projects, full transcript, research. Constraint: no student ID, no invented grades.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark studio portfolio where the academic record is the showpiece: the giant name and portrait open the page, and the transcript is staged as content, not hidden in a PDF. Refuses the plain résumé page with a skills cloud.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black graphite ground (#0d0e10) with a cool slate spotlight taken from the portrait's backdrop; silver-white type; one cold accent (pale steel-blue) for grades and links. Tall condensed display (Big Shoulders Display) in silver, Hanken Grotesk for body, Azeret Mono only for grades and data. Hairline 1px panels, 14px radii, pill buttons.

STORY: The visitor sees who he is and his GPA in seconds, scans three projects, reads the research, checks every grade, then emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav top (monogram OD, links, "Let's talk" pill). The name OHAD / DAHAN at the largest size the viewport allows, set left, with the cut-out portrait standing in front of the second line on the right, lit by the slate spotlight. Under the name: one-line pitch, "View my work" primary pill + "Download CV". Bottom right: "Open to student SWE roles". Stats strip begins at the fold.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — user: "take inspo from the designs in my downloads folder" and chose "Dark (the 'AV' one)".

Signature interaction: transcript sorts between semester view and grade-ranked view; grade bars draw in once on first reveal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
